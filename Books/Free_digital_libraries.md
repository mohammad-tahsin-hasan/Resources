# Free Books, eBooks, Digital Libraries & Audiobooks

A curated collection of websites for discovering and accessing public-domain books, free eBooks, technical learning materials, and audiobooks.

> **Note:** Free access, download options, and lending conditions may vary by title and region. Always check the rights and terms shown by each platform.

## Usage & Copyright Reminder

- Check each book's copyright status before downloading, sharing, or redistributing it.
- A book being available online does not necessarily mean it is in the public domain.
- Some platforms offer free previews, limited lending, or selected free titles rather than unrestricted downloads.
- Follow each website's terms of service and applicable copyright laws.
